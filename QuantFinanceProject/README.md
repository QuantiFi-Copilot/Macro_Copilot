# QuantiFi Copilot: AI Copilot for Indian Equity & Macro Research  

### Overview  
**QuantiFi** is building an intelligent research copilot for the Indian equity and macroeconomic ecosystem.  
The platform automates how analysts gather, interpret, and visualize financial information — combining data engineering, large language models, and time-series analytics to streamline institutional-grade research.  

---

### What We’re Building  
QuantiFi integrates multiple intelligent “agents” designed for different stages of financial analysis:  

- **Earnings Agent:** Automates extraction, parsing, and normalization of corporate filings and results across industries.  
- **Macro Playbook Agent:** Interprets macroeconomic data releases and maps causal sector impacts.  
- **Market Data Agent:** Tracks and analyzes high-frequency market data through TimescaleDB pipelines.  
- **Corporate Agent:** Consolidates corporate actions, shareholding, and disclosure data.  

Each agent operates within a unified data layer and reasoning framework, enabling structured interpretation, cross-domain correlations, and AI-assisted insight generation.  

---

### Vision  
QuantiFi aims to become the analytical backbone for professional research — enabling faster, unbiased, and scalable financial analysis across public and private markets in India.  
Our long-term goal is to create an **agentic financial intelligence layer** capable of autonomously reading data, reasoning over it, and producing research-grade outputs.  

---

### Current Focus  
The current phase centers on the data pipelines — developing a scalable, industry-agnostic data pipelines for public company earnings, macroeconomic data, market data.
This pipeline will form the foundation of QuantiFi’s broader analytical ecosystem, connecting structured data to intelligent reasoning systems.
